Archive settings

A permanent vault

Visits and download records are stored on this device, in this browser profile. There is no automatic deletion. Clearing the browser’s history does not clear this archive. Uninstalling this extension or deleting its profile does.

Existing history is imported once on installation. History already removed by the browser cannot be recovered. Recording requires the extension to be enabled. Private/incognito browsing is excluded.

Domain blacklist

Exclude a domain and all its subdomains. Adding a rule also deletes existing matching archived visits and download records, and matching URLs from browser history. Native download records and files stay intact. Browser history entries may briefly appear before removal; this is not an incognito feature.

Backup & restore

Export regularly to protect your archive. Backups are plain JSON files containing private URLs, titles, dates, and download paths. Store them somewhere you trust.

Restore merges records without duplicates and respects your current blacklist. It can restore entries deleted since the backup. Blacklist rules in the file are kept for reference; add them manually if needed. Downloaded files are not backed up.

Storage & cleanup

The sidebar shows estimated disk use for this extension, including database overhead and small deletion markers that prevent removed records from returning during import. The browser manages database compaction, so disk use may not fall immediately after deletion.

About The Vault

Version 1.15.0 · No analytics or remote scripts. Search looks through saved titles and addresses, not webpage contents. Website icons come from the browser’s favicon service. Tabs from other devices require your browser’s own sync. Download history stores metadata only.
